# Nothing outranks the sort a preset asks for

Under `recent-30d` — a preset whose entire purpose is "the last thirty days, newest first" — the first ten rows of page A were dated August 21, August 20, August 16, August 16, August 15, August 10, August 3, July 28, July 27, then an August 6 row, and only then did the date order begin. Every one of those rows was correct by the comparator: [ADR-0210](0210-work-rows-interleave-across-kinds-by-creation-date.md) made creation date the cross-kind key but kept the three membership tiers above it, and `work.Tier` ranks live-drain, then auto-drain, then orphaned, then everything else. The `sort` field was resolving fine; the tiers were simply louder than it.

The tiers were not equally at fault. Live-drain and orphaned are re-derived every build from the drain snapshot and the filesystem, so a row floats for as long as the fact holds and then stops. **Auto-drain is a persisted bit on the Work container registry row** — a scheduling grant a human switched on once, which survives DONE, verification and archiving. It accumulates: every set that was ever auto-drained floats above every set that never was, forever, and after a few months of use that block is the whole first screen. ADR-0210 priced the tiers as "a live drain is the one row a human always needs to see"; it did not price the tier next to it, which is not a fact about now at all.

The decision: **the active Work view preset's `sort` is the whole of the row order, and the membership tiers are retired as an ordering concept.** `work.Tier` and `TierRunning`/`TierAutoDrain`/`TierOrphaned`/`TierRest` are deleted; the tier check leaves `work.crossKindLess` and `tasks.WorkRowLess`. `LiveDrain`, `AutoDrain` and `Orphaned` stay on `work.Container`, read by the STATUS cell exactly as before — the facts are still on the row, they just no longer decide where the row sits. A live drain is found by its indicator and by being today's work, which is where `created_desc` already puts it.

Two alternatives were considered and rejected, both variants of keeping the float behind a gate. **A per-preset grant** — a `float` bool beside `pin`, declared by `active` alone — was the first plan and is the more defensible of the two, since it maps onto the pane-pin precedent and leaves the default view untouched. It was dropped for being more machinery than the thing it protects: a config field, a validation rule, a `hide`-clause rejection, a copy in `unfilteredDeps`, a glossary clause on **Work view preset**, and two orderings coexisting in the codebase forever — all to preserve a lift that `pin` already provides for the pane you are standing in. **Narrowing the auto-drain tier to sets whose auto-drain can still fire** fixes the accumulation and nothing else; it leaves a date view that still reorders rows for reasons that are not dates, and it adds a status predicate to a comparator that had no business reading status under `created_desc`.

## Consequences

- **A finished, verified July set with auto-drain still on no longer sits above today's work.** This is the whole point; it is also the one behaviour someone may miss, since that block doubled as an informal "sets pop manages" list. The `auto-drain` STATUS suffix and `--preset all` are the routes to it.
- **`work.Tier` had no other caller, so the concept is gone rather than relocated.** The plan had been to keep it for scheduling, on the reading that the supervisor's serial dispatch inherited its tiers. It does not: [ADR-0210](0210-work-rows-interleave-across-kinds-by-creation-date.md) already gave scheduling `AdvancersInKindPrecedence` precisely so a display default could not move the checkout ledger's tiebreak, and a kind's `Candidates()` never consults a tier. Nothing about pick-up order changes here — there was no coupling to sever.
- **A live drain in another pane's checkout loses its guaranteed top slot.** It keeps the pane pin when it is *your* pane's work (a grant of the `active` preset), the live-drain indicator wherever it renders, and its date position — which for a running drain is almost always the top of a `created_desc` page anyway.
- **`sort: status` is unchanged below the tiers.** The bands, the per-project grouping and the explicit status order are exactly as [ADR-0121](0121-work-supervision-read-surfaces-converge-hide-done-and-sort-by-status.md) left them; only the float above them is gone. A live-drained READY set still reads IN PROGRESS and still leads, because the band says so.
- **Amends [ADR-0210](0210-work-rows-interleave-across-kinds-by-creation-date.md).** Its comparator "membership tier, then `CreatedAt` descending, then kind precedence, then the owning kind's `Less`" loses its first key, and its consequence that "the membership tiers survive untouched above the date key" is reversed. The rest of that ADR — the cross-kind date key, zero-means-no-opinion, the validated Map date prefix, `sort: status` as the opt-in — stands.
- **Amends [ADR-0121](0121-work-supervision-read-surfaces-converge-hide-done-and-sort-by-status.md).** The membership tiers it introduced above the status scheme are retired; the scheme itself is untouched.
- Glossary: **Work surface sort order** and **Work kind** are redefined; no term is added, and the tier vocabulary moves to `_Avoid_`.
