+ Forced verification
  A **Verifier** run a human starts directly — `pop tasks verify <set>` or the HITL gate's Re-verify — rather than the tail of a **Drain**. It always invokes the Verifier, overwrites the **Verify verdict** at the current work SHA and publishes a **Verify report**, but it enacts nothing on its own: no **Remediation task** is spawned and no set is parked. On a TTY a non-PASS forced verdict ends at the **Verify-fail gate prompt**, so the human who asked for the judgment dispositions it in the same sitting — Accept, Remediate, assistance, shell or exit — and every non-PASS verdict, FIXABLE under the cap included, reaches that gate because no drain is present to act on it. Headless, or when the human exits the gate, the verdict stays cached and the next Drain of the set enacts it under the ordinary **Verify verdict disposition**: a cached FIXABLE spawns a Remediation task without a second Verifier run. On a **Human completion** the forced verdict lands only as a **Verification mark** and no gate is offered, matching the drain. Editing a task's acceptance criteria does not invalidate a forced verdict — the work SHA does not move — so a human who changes the criteria re-runs the verification or records an **Accepted verdict** before the next drain, or the drain remediates stale findings.

  under: Agent verification

~ Verify verdict disposition
  How each three-way **Verify verdict** drives what happens next. **PASS** immunizes: no further automatic **Verifier** runs in the episode (**Verification idempotency after PASS**). **FIXABLE** spawns a **Remediation task**, **Verification invalidation** clears the cache, and re-verify is mandatory after remediation drains — a deliberate loop, not a failure retry. **NEEDS-HUMAN** (or exhausted remediation cap) parks at VERIFY-FAILED; the prior non-PASS verdict warrants re-verify on the next terminal drain attempt. On a set carrying a **Human completion** every non-PASS disposition is suspended: the verdict is still run, recorded and printed, but it neither parks the set nor spawns a **Remediation task** — spawning fix work would reopen work the human closed — so the finding lands only as a **Verification mark**. For the same reason an **Agent quota pause** during the set's verify phase is reported and dropped instead of entering an **Agent quota recovery wait**: no verdict it could produce would change the terminal, so the drain falls through with the mark left `unverified` and the **Verifier** still scheduled for a later drain. A **Forced verification** sits outside this automatic disposition: it records the verdict and hands a non-PASS to the human at the **Verify-fail gate prompt**, and only a later Drain enacts what the human left cached.
